# Bracket intake — workers' guide (DRAFT)

Every tournament, boys and girls, goes through the same three steps in **Admin → Tournament Hub**. The hub has five
tabs: **In-season tournaments · Team postseason · District · Region · State**. Open the tournament, then work the steps
in order. This text will appear on the hub page beside each step.

---

## Step 1 — Load the blank bracket (before the event)

Do this as soon as the brackets are posted (postseason: after the seeding meetings, usually the night before).

**Get the bracket — one PDF for the whole tournament**
1. Open the tournament's bracket page on its site (TrackWrestling, FloWrestling, or whoever runs it), showing all weights.
2. **Print it to PDF:** press **⌘P** (Mac) or **Ctrl+P** (Windows) → set the destination to **Save as PDF** → Save.
   One PDF with every weight is fine (one weight per page). This works on every site, including FloWrestling (which
   doesn't let you save the page itself).

**Load it**
1. In the hub, open the tournament → **Load bracket**.
2. Drag in the PDF.
3. **If the page asks for the text of a weight** (it couldn't read that weight cleanly): open that weight's bracket
   on the site, press **⌘A** / **Ctrl+A**, **⌘C** / **Ctrl+C**, and paste it into the box it shows. Only when asked.
4. The page shows each weight's bracket as it will look on the site, and lists anything it couldn't settle. Each item
   sits **next to that section of the PDF**, zoomed in, so you can decide at a glance:
   - a name it couldn't match to a wrestler → pick the right wrestler, or **New wrestler** if they truly aren't in the system
   - a bracket whose number of lines is off → re-print that weight and load it again
   - an entrant who doesn't belong (e.g. not a district placer at regions) → check the bracket and tell Kyle
5. When the list is empty, click **Approve**. The full bracket goes live on the site with empty cards for the bouts.

**If the bracket changes** (a scratch, an alternate stepping in): print the changed weights again and load them.
The page shows exactly what changed (e.g. "line 7: Smith → Jones") and asks before applying it.

---

## Step 2 — Load results (during and after)

1. Download the results file from the tournament site (TrackWrestling: the results export / CSV), or copy the round
   results text.
2. In the hub, open the tournament → **Load results** → drag in the file (or paste the text).
3. Every bout is placed on the bracket by its two wrestlers. **If everything fits, it's saved — done.**
4. **If anything doesn't fit**, the page asks for the tournament's **completed brackets**: print the whole tournament
   to PDF again (now that the bouts are in) and drop it in. Then each problem sits **next to its section of the
   completed bracket**, zoomed in, and says why it's there:
   - **a name that isn't on the bracket** → pick which bracket wrestler it is (the choices are listed)
   - **wrestlers who don't meet in that round** → wrong weight, wrong round label, or a bracket that changed: correct
     it from the PDF, or re-load that weight's blank bracket if it changed
   - **who won doesn't match the bracket's path**, **a result that can't be right** (a major with a 3-point margin, a
     pin past 6:00), or **the same bout twice with different results** → pick the right one from the PDF
   There is no "skip": you settle each one from the PDF. Nothing that doesn't fit is saved until it's settled.
5. You can load results as often as you like (after each session); loading the same bout twice never duplicates it.

---

## Step 3 — Close out (after the last session)

1. Open the tournament → **Close out**.
2. The page lists any bout still missing, by weight and place on the bracket (e.g. "120 — consolation semifinal,
   line 5 vs line 12"). Load results again or check the site for those.
   A tournament can't be closed while anything is on the **Needs review** list or a bout is missing.
3. Copy the team standings from TrackWrestling into the **Team scores** box (TrackWrestling's standings are the
   official numbers).
4. Click **Close out**. The tournament is marked complete.

---

### Good to know
- Seeds printed on a bracket are shown on the site, but the bracket's **lines** decide who wrestles whom — committees
  don't always seed in order (a district champ can be seeded below a runner-up; byes aren't always even).
- A results export lists bouts in no particular order. That's fine once the blank bracket is loaded.
- Never load results for a tournament whose bracket hasn't been loaded; the hub will ask for the bracket first.
