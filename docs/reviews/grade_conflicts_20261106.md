# Grade conflicts (2026-11-06)

Wrestlers whose profile grade and the grades on their own tournament entries point to different graduation years. No graduation year is set for these; review each one.

**How to read a line:** `grade Jr [roster_upload] → class of 2027; entries say 2026` means the profile says Junior, from a roster upload. As a 2025-26 Junior that's the class of 2027. But the grade printed on the wrestler's district/region/state entries implies the class of 2026.

**Where the profile grade came from** (`grade_confirmed_source`):
- **roster_upload**: the TrackWrestling team rosters with grades (the 2025-26 rosters Kyle uploaded), taken when an admin confirms a match in Roster Grade Import, or when a wrestler is created or split from a roster row. Treated as a 2025-26 grade.
- **manual**: typed or picked by an admin. This covers the grade field on the admin wrestler page, choosing a grade by hand in Roster Grade Import (a wrestler with no roster row), and an unresolved-grade fix. Season Rollover also records "manual", but it has never run. Treated as a 2025-26 grade.
- **districts**: copied from the wrestler's district entry (the 2026-09-13 fill). It belongs to that entry's season.
- **import_review**: chosen in a dual or tournament import review, without a roster row.

**Entries**: the grade printed next to the wrestler on the NJSIAA district, region and state brackets, for each season they entered. Per our rule, records outrank TrackWrestling rosters, so in most of these lines the entries are probably right and the profile grade is a year off.
